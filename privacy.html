Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Isravel Backup is used only by Isravel Department Store to store its own encrypted backups in the store owner's Google Drive.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive permission drive.file. With it, the app can only create, see and delete the files it creates itself (in a folder named "Isravel Secure Backup"). It cannot see or open any other files in the Google Drive account.

What is stored

Only the store's own backup files, encrypted with AES-256 on the store's server before upload. They can only be opened with the store owner's backup password, which is never sent to Google or to anyone else.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from the store's dashboard or from their Google Account (Security → Third-party connections). Backup files in the folder can be deleted from Google Drive at any time.
